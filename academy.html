About GenT4Tech Academy

Empowering the next generation of tech professionals

Our Mission: To empower individuals from all backgrounds with premium, practical, and purpose-driven tech education — transforming complete beginners into industry-ready professionals through mentorship, innovation, and community-based learning.

At GenT4Tech Academy, we believe that everyone has the potential to excel in technology. We provide comprehensive training in web development, mobile development, cybersecurity, data analysis, QA testing, and UI/UX design. Our experienced and certified tutors deliver hands-on projects, real-world solutions, and personalized mentorship to ensure your success.

With a passion for education and technology, P. David founded GenT4Tech Academy to bridge the gap between aspiring tech professionals and industry demands.
